**[Indistinct](https://indistinct.ai)** — A deployment practice for operators who know AI is unavoidable but don't have the time or personnel to plug in the stack themselves. The intelligence layer is getting built by Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google. None of them will walk into a business and operationalize it. Indistinct closes that gap. Family offices, founders, chiefs of staff — wherever one person's leverage is worth everything.

**Virgil** — Indistinct's flagship. A persistent AI operating system that holds context, relationships, priorities, and active threads across conversations, not within one. Built on Claude. Lives on infrastructure the client owns — every artifact written back to a private repository, nothing on our servers. The name was chosen by Claude when given the problem it was built to solve: in Dante's *Inferno*, Virgil is the guide who walks you through every layer of complexity below, but cannot enter Heaven. Reason takes you to the threshold. The decision past it is yours. Virgil guides. You decide.

**Where it's going** — Real-time relationship intelligence in chat. The operator talks naturally about people, deals, decisions, what's moving; Virgil captures who's who and what's connected as it surfaces, writes it back to the graph mid-conversation, and pulls the right context forward before the next ask. Contradictions surface when state changes. Stale facts age out. The current build edge is the bridge between operator speech and structured relationship data — what most products treat as data entry, Virgil treats as a conversation problem.
