[Indistinct](https://indistinct.ai/) - AI that holds what you know. The intelligence layer is getting built by Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google. None of them will hold your world - the people, deals, decisions, and open threads that make your operation yours. Indistinct closes that gap: durable private context inside the AI you already use, that stays yours and goes where you go. Product-first, alongside a hands-on practice for family offices, founders, and chiefs of staff - wherever one person's leverage is worth everything.

**Virgil** - Indistinct's flagship. A persistent brain that works inside Claude and ChatGPT at once: talk naturally and it captures who's who and what's connected, writes it to a knowledge graph mid-conversation, and pulls the right context forward before the next ask. No new app, nothing to tag, nothing to manage. Switch models whenever you want and your memory doesn't reset - it isn't a feature locked inside someone else's product, it's the one part of your stack that's actually yours, and it can't be switched off, walled in, or left behind. When facts change, the old record is superseded, never deleted - the brain keeps receipts. Ask it how it knows something and it tells you: source, date, confidence. The graph-memory core is built on [gbrain](https://github.com/garrytan/gbrain), Garry Tan's open-source engine, which Indistinct runs in production and extends - multi-tenant isolation, measured formation, reconciliation, and the cross-model connector are ours, with fixes contributed back upstream. Every artifact is written to a private repository - the record is the customer's, fully auditable, built to leave with them.

**Memvelope** - The open standard underneath the promise. One portable JSON envelope for any AI chat export (ChatGPT, Claude) - MIT-licensed, converts in the browser at [memvelope.com](https://memvelope.com) with nothing uploaded, zero-dependency CLI on npm (`npx memvelope`). The gbrain importer is contributed upstream ([garrytan/gbrain #2568](https://github.com/garrytan/gbrain/pull/2568)) - any chat export can now cold-start a brain. Live-verified at scale: a real 662MB export → 353 conversations → 353 distinct pages, provenance and message-id citations intact. "Your memory is yours" is a download button, not a slogan.

**Measured, not asserted** - Formation quality is benchmarked, not vibes: N=92 operator-adjudicated cases, detection precision 88% → 97.1% across the last hardening cycle, every label carrying its provenance. The benchmark grows itself - production corrections land as new labeled cases at the moment they're made. Each fact carries where it came from - what you actually said, or a clearly-marked inference, never a guess - and the day it landed. It's a structured record your AI pulls the exact relevant piece from, not the wall of text most "AI memory" really is. All of it exportable anytime, in a format you keep. The interchange format holds to the same bar - conformance-tested to byte-for-byte determinism, including a timezone matrix that runs through Chatham Island.

**Where it's going** - Launch on the Claude Connectors Directory and the ChatGPT app store, both submitted and in review. Full brain export in the Memvelope standard - the entire graph, supersession chains and confidence included, in the same open format that already ships for chat history. A time axis no other AI memory has: what changed this month, what did I believe in March, what do I owe people and what's slipping. The bet is simple: the model providers build the summary. Virgil builds the record.
